隐私政策

最近更新：2026 年 11 月 20 日。本政策说明「概念网络 - 思维模型」（英文名 ConceptFlow: Mental Models，以下简称"本应用"）在 iOS 与 Android 上如何处理你的信息。

Bundle ID: com.juxianyp.conceptflow 无需注册账号 无广告 · 无追踪

概述

本应用是一款概念学习与认知训练工具：你可以搜索或浏览一个概念，查看它的解释、原理和应用，在概念网络中展开相关概念，做每日练习，并记录自己的笔记。

本应用不需要注册账号，不展示广告，不参与跨 App 追踪，也不会出售你的个人信息。

存储在你设备上的信息

以下内容默认只保存在你的设备本地（SQLite / 本地存储），用于提供应用功能：

  • 你创建或浏览的概念网络、概念卡片及其生成内容
  • 笔记（文字、你添加的图片和语音笔记）、收藏、最近查看记录
  • 练习记录、复习进度与学习偏好
  • 应用设置；如你自行填写了 AI 服务的 API 密钥，它会保存在系统安全存储（Keychain / Keystore）中

发送到 AI 服务的信息

在支持 AI 功能的版本中，只有你主动使用相关功能时，所需内容才会通过加密连接发送给 AI 服务进行实时处理：

  • 文字内容：你搜索的概念、发起的提问、对话内容、练习中的回答，用于生成概念解释、关联概念、练习与反馈
  • 图片：你主动添加到笔记并请求分析的图片
  • 语音：仅在你开启"实时语音对话"时，麦克风音频会实时传输给 AI 服务用于语音对话

这些请求可能由开发者运营的服务端（Photon）转发处理，或发送给第三方模型服务商（如 Google Gemini、OpenRouter），具体取决于你在设置中选择的模型。这些内容仅用于完成你当次请求的功能，不与你的身份关联，也不用于广告或追踪。第三方服务商对数据的处理同时受其各自隐私政策约束。

请不要在提问、笔记或语音中输入你不希望发送给 AI 服务的敏感个人信息。

App Store 购买与订阅

在 iOS 上，Apple 处理 App Store 的付款。ConceptFlow 使用 RevenueCat 核验购买、管理会员权益、恢复购买和提供购买支持。RevenueCat 会处理应用生成的匿名用户标识，以及 App Store 提供的交易凭据、商品、购买和续订或到期状态。ConceptFlow 和 RevenueCat 不会收到你的银行卡号。

这些数据用于提供购买相关功能，不用于广告或跨 App 追踪。RevenueCat 按其 隐私政策 保护和处理数据。你可以在 Apple 账户设置中管理或取消订阅。

权限

  • 麦克风：在你主动录制语音笔记时使用。如果当前版本提供语音对话，也会在你主动开启对话时使用
  • 照片：用于读取你为笔记选中的图片，或按你的操作保存分享卡片到相册
  • 网络：用于核验和恢复 App Store 购买，以及在你使用适用的 AI 功能时处理请求

你可以随时在系统设置中关闭这些权限，关闭后对应功能将不可用，其他功能不受影响。

数据保留与删除

本地数据由你完全控制：你可以在应用内删除概念网络、笔记等内容；卸载应用会清除应用保存在设备上的全部数据。开发者不在服务器上保存你的个人资料库。

购买记录会在核验和恢复会员资格、处理争议或履行法定留存义务所需期间保留。你可以通过本页联系方式请求删除由我们管理的购买记录。Apple 账户中的交易记录由 Apple 管理。

未成年人

本应用面向一般学习者，不针对 13 岁以下儿童设计，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

AI 内容说明

AI 生成的内容可能存在错误或不完整，仅供学习参考，不构成医疗、法律、财务等专业建议。

政策变更

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改顶部的"最近更新"日期。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: November 20, 2026. ConceptFlow: Mental Models ("the app") is a concept-learning and thinking-practice tool. It does not require an account, shows no ads, does not use cross-app tracking, and does not sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

  • Concept networks, concept cards and their generated content
  • Notes (text, images you add, and voice recordings), favorites, and recently viewed items
  • Practice history, review progress and learning preferences
  • App settings; an AI API key you enter yourself is kept in the system secure storage (Keychain / Keystore)

Information sent to AI services

In versions that offer AI features, relevant content is sent over an encrypted connection for real-time processing only when you choose to use those features:

  • Text: concepts you search, questions, conversations and practice answers
  • Images: images you add to a note and ask the app to analyze
  • Voice: microphone audio, only while you have live voice chat turned on

Requests may be relayed by the developer-operated server (Photon) or sent to third-party model providers such as Google Gemini or OpenRouter, depending on the model selected in settings. This content is used only to fulfill your request, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or tracking. Third-party providers also process data under their own privacy policies. Please avoid entering sensitive personal information you do not want sent to an AI service.

App Store purchases and subscriptions

On iOS, Apple processes App Store payments. ConceptFlow uses RevenueCat to verify purchases, manage membership access, restore purchases, and provide purchase support. RevenueCat processes an app-generated anonymous user ID and transaction information supplied by the App Store, including receipts, products, purchases, and renewal or expiration status. ConceptFlow and RevenueCat do not receive your card number.

We use this data for purchase-related app functionality. We do not use it for advertising or cross-app tracking. RevenueCat protects and handles this data under its Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Permissions

  • Microphone: used when you record a voice note or start voice chat in a version that offers it
  • Photos: used to read images you select for notes or save share cards to your photo library when you choose
  • Network: used to verify and restore App Store purchases and handle requests when you use available AI features

Retention, deletion and children

Your local data stays under your control: delete items inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its on-device data. The developer does not keep your personal library on a server. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information. AI-generated content may be inaccurate and is not professional medical, legal or financial advice.

We retain purchase records as needed to verify and restore membership, resolve disputes, or meet legal retention obligations. Use the contact details on this page to request deletion of purchase records that we manage. Apple manages transactions in your Apple account.
