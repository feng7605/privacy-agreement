Privacy Policy for Sonic Lens

Last updated: April 8, 2026. This policy describes how Sonic Lens handles information when you use the app on iOS or Android.

App name: Sonic Lens Bundle ID: com.juxianyp.picktune Account not required

Overview

Sonic Lens is a playlist-first audio player for local audio, direct online audio links, offline downloads, and on-device subtitle extraction for local audio. The app is designed around local storage first. Most of the information it uses to function is stored on your device.

Sonic Lens does not require you to create an account to use its core features, and the app is not built around cloud syncing of your personal library.

Information stored on your device

Sonic Lens may store the following information locally on your device to provide app functionality:

  • audio library records, playlists, and playlist ordering
  • track metadata such as title, artist, album, folder, genre, and source link where applicable
  • recent playback activity, continue listening state, playback queue, and playback preferences
  • download task records, file targets, retry state, and local file paths
  • subtitle documents, subtitle jobs, model state, exported subtitle references, and subtitle preferences
  • private space settings, PIN-related state, and privacy display preferences
  • theme, language, scanning, and other app settings

Information you choose to provide

You may choose to provide Sonic Lens with local audio files, selected folders, direct audio links, playlist names, custom tags, subtitle exports, and other content you intentionally add to the app.

If you move audio into the private space, that content is still stored on your device but handled with additional in-app privacy controls.

How network access is used

Sonic Lens uses network access only when a feature needs it. Examples include:

  • playing a direct online audio link that you provide
  • downloading online audio to your device when you request it
  • downloading subtitle models used for on-device transcription
  • checking update or version information when update functionality is used
  • sending limited app usage and screen analytics to Firebase Analytics to help improve app quality and feature decisions

Sonic Lens is not designed as an account-based cloud service. Core library, playback, playlist, and subtitle state are not intended to depend on remote account storage.

Subtitle processing

For supported local audio, Sonic Lens can generate subtitles after you download a compatible subtitle model. Based on the current app implementation, subtitle extraction for local audio is handled on device.

Generated subtitle content, segment timing, export files, and related subtitle state may be stored locally so you can review transcripts, jump by timestamp, retry a transcription job, or export SRT and VTT files.

Permissions

Sonic Lens follows an action-based permission model. It does not request unrelated runtime permissions on first launch. Relevant permissions or system access may be used as follows:

  • File or folder access: used when you explicitly import files or choose a directory through a system picker
  • Notifications on Android 13+: requested when playback starts so media notifications and controls can be shown
  • Internet and network state: used for direct link playback, downloads, subtitle model downloads, and related network-dependent features

The app currently does not proactively request camera, microphone, contacts, photo library, or broad media-library access for its primary workflows.

Private space

Sonic Lens includes a PIN-protected private space for audio you do not want exposed in normal browsing surfaces. Private items can also be masked in certain playback-facing contexts based on your settings.

The private space is an app-level privacy feature. You remain responsible for the overall security of your device, backups, and any exported files outside the app.

Data sharing and third parties

Sonic Lens is not built around account syncing or ad networks for the core app experience. The app does not describe selling your personal information.

Some network requests may necessarily involve third-party servers that host audio you choose to play, files you choose to download, subtitle models you choose to download, or platform distribution and update infrastructure.

Sonic Lens now uses Firebase Analytics, a Google service, for limited product analytics. This may include app launches, screen views, feature interactions, app version, device platform, and other aggregated usage signals that help us understand stability and feature adoption. Sonic Lens is not intended to send your private audio files, local file paths, subtitle text, private-space contents, or exported media content to Firebase Analytics as part of normal analytics collection.

Data retention and deletion

Because Sonic Lens primarily stores its working data on your device, retention generally depends on how long you keep the app, the local files you import, and the content you choose to download or export.

You can remove downloads, subtitle exports, playlist items, and other library content from inside the app. Removing the app may also remove locally stored app data, subject to your platform's behavior and any external backups you manage.

Children's privacy

Sonic Lens is a general audio utility and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe personal information has been provided in a way that should be reviewed or removed, contact us at feng7605@gmail.com.
