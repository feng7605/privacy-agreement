Core experience

Built for listening that lasts longer than one tap.

Sonic Lens is not a streaming catalog. It is the place where your own audio, saved links, playlists, and transcripts stay organized over time.

01 — Library

Keep local and linked audio together

Import local files, scan directories, add direct audio URLs, and manage songs, artists, albums, folders, genres, downloads, and playlists in one library.

02 — Playback

Stay in one playback system

Queue management, playback speed, playback modes, sleep timer, recent plays, and continue listening all work together so the app remembers your context.

03 — Subtitles

Turn local audio into text on device

Download a subtitle model, transcribe local audio on device, review the full transcript, jump by timestamp, and export subtitles as SRT or VTT.

Playlist-first by design

Sonic Lens treats playlists as the stable structure behind playback. What you play, save, download, and return to stays inside one consistent listening flow, instead of becoming scattered one-off items.

Listen now, decide later

Found a direct audio link? Start listening immediately, then decide whether it belongs in your offline library. Downloads and local saves stay connected to the same track identity when possible.

Private audio stays private

Sensitive content can be moved into a PIN-protected private space. Hidden content can stay out of normal browsing surfaces and can be masked in system-facing media displays.

Designed for collected audio

Podcasts, interviews, voice notes, courses, archive recordings, and personal audio favorites all fit naturally because the app is built around keeping and revisiting what you listen to.

Privacy

Your library is built around on-device storage first.

Sonic Lens does not require an account to use the core app. Audio metadata, playlists, playback state, downloads, subtitle records, and app preferences are primarily stored on your device.

On-device data

Library and playback state stay local

The app stores library records, playlists, recent activity, subtitle records, privacy settings, and playback preferences on your device using local app storage.

Network usage

Only when a feature needs it

Network access is used for direct audio links, downloads, subtitle model downloads, and version or update checks. The app does not require cloud sync for its core behavior.

Subtitle extraction

Processed on device for local audio

After you download a supported subtitle model, transcription for local audio is handled on device. Export remains under your control.
